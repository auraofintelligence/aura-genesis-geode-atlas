HARDWARE / THE COMPARISON

Four ways into the Geode.

A conventional chamber provides one research integration route. Three Geode generations develop mineral structure, crystalline architecture and eventually material-integrated computation.

01 / Four pathways

A family of configurations.

Hardware, personal data and the participant experience have connected histories, but each configuration needs its own equipment record, operating review and evidence. A successful software demonstration does not qualify a material, pressure assembly or clinical use.

02 / Four pathways

An active self-reflection workspace.

The current project brief reserves usable space for haptic touchscreens or VR/XR interaction, alongside the participant and their wearable sensors. The seated person needs to reach the interface, move comfortably and review their own records. Guided rest is one part of the protocol; the layout also supports deliberate interaction, reflection and learning.

  • A reachable adjustable display and a defined VR/XR use envelope, with a headset dock beside the seat.
  • A serviceable sensor connection bay, cable management and provision for a compatible wireless receiver where selected.
  • Screens and accessories stow alongside the seat, keeping the entry, assisted-transfer route and door operation clear.
  • The complete equipment configuration requires review for the actual chamber environment; experience interfaces remain separate from independent chamber controls.
03 / Four pathways

Shared records. Different engineering questions.

The same category identities, source links and permissions are intended to travel between configurations. The pressure boundary, inhabited lining, displays, acquisition equipment and compute each retain an explicit assigned duty.

  • Conventional retrofit · manufacturer-specific integration and compatibility.
  • Geopolymer Geode · cast mineral recipes, panels, joints and repeatable fabrication.
  • Crystalline, fused-quartz & blend Geode · phase identity, bonding, optical and acoustic behaviour.
  • Computational crystalline Geode · tiles, routing, integrated layers and atomic-precision specimens.
04 / Four pathways

Evidence moves a pathway forward.

A material specimen advances through measured tests to an interface sample, a panel and a complete assembly. A software capability advances through a defined task, permission tests and participant review. Neither sequence carries an assumed deployment date.

05 / Four pathways

Emergency egress is an acceptance gate.

Entry, door operation, assisted transfer and emergency extraction must conform to the applicable requirements for the actual chamber and site. The project retains dimensioned evidence, a reviewed emergency procedure and rehearsed acceptance before participant use. The dedicated entry and egress chapter follows this requirement across every pathway.
