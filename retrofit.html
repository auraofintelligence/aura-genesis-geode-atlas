02 / Four pathways

An active self-reflection workspace.

The current project brief reserves usable space for haptic touchscreens or VR/XR interaction, alongside the participant and their wearable sensors. The seated person needs to reach the interface, move comfortably and review their own records. Guided rest is one part of the protocol; the layout also supports deliberate interaction, reflection and learning.

  • A reachable adjustable display and a defined VR/XR use envelope, with a headset dock beside the seat.
  • A serviceable sensor connection bay, cable management and provision for a compatible wireless receiver where selected.
  • Screens and accessories stow alongside the seat, keeping the entry, assisted-transfer route and door operation clear.
  • The complete equipment configuration requires review for the actual chamber environment; experience interfaces remain separate from independent chamber controls.
03 / Four pathways

The chamber has its own identity.

The starting dossier names the manufacturer, model, intended use, service history, installation, instructions for use and actual operating team. The public atlas does not nominate a product or imply that an available chamber is approved for every proposed experience.

04 / Four pathways

Integration begins outside the pressure boundary.

An ordinary-room rehearsal develops the category interface, dialogue, accessible controls and source records. The proposed architecture keeps the validated chamber controller independent from general AI and game presentation. Operating records reach the personal system through a read-only interface.

  • External compute and acquisition resources have separate workloads and failure plans.
  • Any internal display, sensor, cable, textile, lining or fixture needs configuration-specific compatibility review.
  • The Personal Atmosphere Delivery System (PADS) remains a development option with its own circuit, controller and operating evidence.
05 / Four pathways

A retrofit is an engineering project.

Changes to an existing device require the relevant manufacturer, technical, clinical and regulatory review. The Australian Therapeutic Goods Administration links hyperbaric fire prevention to the device instructions, qualified professionals and the operating environment.

Sources & contextTGA · Risk of fire during use of hyperbaric chambers
06 / Four pathways

What counts as a useful first result?

A non-pressure interface rehearsal demonstrates that a participant retrieves a source, corrects an interpretation and previews a release. A later installed configuration demonstrates timing, capture continuity, comfort and fault recovery under its actual approved procedures.

07 / Four pathways

Emergency egress is an acceptance gate.

Entry, door operation, assisted transfer and emergency extraction must conform to the applicable requirements for the actual chamber and site. The project retains dimensioned evidence, a reviewed emergency procedure and rehearsed acceptance before participant use. The dedicated entry and egress chapter follows this requirement across every pathway.
