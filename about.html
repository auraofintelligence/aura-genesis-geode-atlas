01 / Research & sources

Direction and authorship.

The source documents name Luke Nathan Hayes as design direction for Aura of Intelligence. This site turns that supplied material and the requested fourth hardware route into an explorable editorial structure. It does not report a built Geode, a completed trial or an operating programme.

02 / Research & sources

A related visual identity, made anew.

Deep mineral green, warm ivory, lilac and champagne connect this atlas to its partner family’s luminous colour and serif typography. Four original concept artworks depict compact single-person Geodes. The existing-hardware chapter uses credited manufacturer photography. Partner images and logos were not copied. Diagrams show conceptual relationships rather than surveyed plans or engineering drawings.

03 / Research & sources

Useful boundaries.

This website provides project information and proposed learning experiences. Pressure treatment, equipment modifications and human research follow the actual qualified teams and applicable review. No pressure-setting, oxygen prescription or individual medical recommendation is supplied.

04 / Research & sources

Privacy of the website itself.

The site has no account, analytics, camera, microphone capture or backend. Optional learning bookmarks remain in this browser’s local storage and have a clear reset control. These site bookmarks are separate from the proposed Genesis recording architecture. Manufacturer photographs load directly from their source websites, which receive the usual image requests.

05 / Research & sources

Rights and attribution.

Original source documents and the Aura project identity retain their existing rights. Third-party papers and websites remain with their respective rights holders and are linked with attribution. No open-source licence is inferred for supplied material. The repository includes an explicit rights notice and the image prompt record.
