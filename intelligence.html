From a record to useful action.

01 / Personal intelligence

A personal nanoLLM is a design ambition.

The documents propose a compact language model shaped by approved personal examples. Model size follows task evidence and device resources. The training contract states tasks, source categories, private or secret grants, vocabulary and fidelity criteria.

02 / Personal intelligence

Faithfulness before confidence.

Declaration/reflection pairs, contextual examples and corrections form candidate data. Training and held-out evaluation have separate roles. Tests examine meaning, retrieval, conditions, uncertainty and response to a correction. A version card records method, sources, licence, compute and limits.

03 / Personal intelligence

Functions give action a contract.

A function defines purpose, inputs, outputs and errors. A caller selects and orders tools within permission. An agent has a role and handover boundary. An Aura specialist team and neural mixture-of-experts are different forms of organisation.

04 / Personal intelligence

Three observers open a simulation.

The environment observer describes the wider setting. The character observer describes what the agent notices. A narrator interprets action in context. Their disagreement is useful evidence about attention and assumptions. Simulated choices remain distinct from real-world authority.

05 / Personal intelligence

Compute belongs to a workload.

Personal devices, session workstations and chosen shared nodes handle different jobs. Capture continuity has independent resources. The source’s RAM, latency, storage and power envelopes are planning examples to benchmark, rather than procurement or performance claims.
