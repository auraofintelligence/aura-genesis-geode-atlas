MANDATORY DESIGN GATE / ACCESS AND EMERGENCY EGRESS

Entry and exit shape the chamber.

Every bought, retrofitted or newly built chamber must conform to its applicable emergency-egress requirements. Entry geometry and assisted extraction are part of the core design from the first layout.

01 / Four pathways

A release requirement across all four pathways.

A chamber is not ready for participant use until its actual entry, closure, emergency procedures and assisted egress have been demonstrated against the applicable standards, manufacturer instructions and installation requirements. Material beauty, compact size and software capability do not replace this acceptance record.

02 / Four pathways

Identify the applicable requirements.

The project compliance register is to name the jurisdiction, intended use, chamber classification, standards and editions, relevant clauses, evidence and responsible reviewer. AS/NZS 4774.2:2019 addresses hyperbaric oxygen facilities; ASME PVHO-1 addresses pressure vessels for human occupancy. NFPA 99 is a further hyperbaric-facility code reference. Applicability and any adopted editions are established for the actual design and site; this website does not reproduce the purchased standards or invent clause-level clearances.

03 / Four pathways

The person defines the access envelope.

The design review includes the intended participant’s body size, mobility, transfer support and foreseeable loss of ability to self-exit. A dimensioned full-size mock-up demonstrates door opening, threshold, seat adjustment, helper reach and the extraction route using the intended equipment.

  • No crystal lining, seam, display or compute tile obstructs the required clear opening.
  • No sensor lead, mask hose, clothing attachment or headset traps the participant.
  • Door travel and external equipment preserve the required assisted-transfer area.
  • The room route continues to the receiving care area and the building’s required exit arrangements.
04 / Four pathways

Emergency egress is a complete sequence.

The responsible hyperbaric team defines the pressure-management, door-release, communication and assisted-extraction procedure for the selected chamber. Fault scenarios include loss of power, communication or relevant controls and an occupant unable to move independently. The acceptance test uses that reviewed sequence; this public atlas supplies no improvised depressurisation rate or latch-opening instructions.

Sources & contextTGA · Risk of fire during use of hyperbaric chambers
05 / Four pathways

Evidence before procurement or integration.

A supplier review requires model-specific drawings, instructions, emergency-system descriptions, conformity documentation and evidence relevant to the intended use. Site acceptance records the actual fitted equipment and rehearsed extraction. Delivery access widths describe getting equipment into a building; they do not establish emergency-egress clearances for a person.

06 / Four pathways

Artwork expresses the requirement.

The revised Geode concepts use a compact seated enclosure, a defined large front entry and clear surrounding access. Artwork is a visual brief. The physical opening, closure and all emergency functions still need dimensioned design, verification and signed acceptance before a conformity claim appears.
