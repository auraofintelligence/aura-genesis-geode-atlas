01 / Start here

The person is the point of departure.

Aura Genesis begins with lived experience: memories, relationships, values, creative interests and possible futures. A personal Aura organises these records for review, correction and further reflection inside the Geode.

02 / Start here

An active self-reflection workspace.

The Geode needs usable space for haptic touchscreens or VR/XR, wearable sensors and receiver hardware. The seated participant must be able to reach the controls, move comfortably and review records, with equipment stowed clear of entry and assisted exit.

  • A reachable adjustable display and a defined VR/XR use envelope, with a headset dock beside the seat.
  • A serviceable sensor connection bay, cable management and provision for a compatible wireless receiver where selected.
  • Screens and accessories stow alongside the seat, keeping the entry, assisted-transfer route and door operation clear.
  • The complete equipment configuration requires review for the actual chamber environment; experience interfaces remain separate from independent chamber controls.
03 / Start here

One adventure. Three distinct clocks.

The source programme proposes sixty sessions containing two qualifying hours at pressure each. Daily activities and teacher development are recorded separately from qualifying pressure time.

  • Genesis · 120 qualifying pressure hours in the source design, subject to an individual authorised operating plan.
  • Immersion · sleep, food, relationships, making, movement and chosen contribution.
  • Teacher development · demonstrated learning, transfer, teaching practice and civic stewardship.
04 / Start here

From a personal record to a wider world.

Participants can link memories, revised interpretations and activity outcomes to their personal models. With permission, selected records can support community twins, creative works and Earth and solar-system projects.
