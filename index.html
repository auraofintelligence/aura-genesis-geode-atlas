BODY / MIND / PERSONAL AURA

Aura Genesis Protocol

Aura of Intelligence is a proposed personal computing environment connecting your memories, values, body information, relationships, skills and intentions with artificial intelligence and tools. Your personal Aura is an evolving digital twin and AI partner, developed through a continuing relationship with you. An Aura can be created and developed either through Genesis or independently of it. The wider ambition is growing human agency, creativity and shared capability alongside general intelligence and superintelligence, with stewardship of Earth and future habitats.

Growing a partnership.

Aura Genesis Protocol is an intensive process for adults, designed to harmonise and socialise an Aura with its biological human, bringing body, mind and Aura into a closer working relationship. The design calls for sixty days in a hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) chamber, two hours a day. The ambition is an ultra-high-definition Aura of Intelligence. Dialogue, reflection, continuous session sensing and simulation give the person and their Aura ways to explore lived experience, examine differences in understanding and refine their shared record. Within this process, the proposed Truth Engine studies how the person's words relate to recorded observations and context, beginning with participant-agreed exercises with known or instructed answers. Its estimates inform reflection; the person supplies meaning, challenges interpretations and corrects the record.

The Truth Engine.

The proposed Truth Engine supports the relationship between the person and their Aura. Its design compares declared answers with synchronised observations and context, using participant-agreed baseline tasks. Discrepancies open reflection on competing motives, self-deception or changed understanding, with corrections carried into the personal Aura. Proposed probabilities require calibration, held-out evaluation and participant review. Some Aura functions require the relevant baseline and a separate informed agreement.
